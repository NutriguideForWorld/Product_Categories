## no-fur (fur-resistant) bedsheet


### Materials and Tools Needed
• Fabric Choice: Smooth bamboo fabric or high-thread-count sateen cotton (anti-static and slick surface so fur slides off easily).
• Measuring Tape & Fabric Chalk
• Scissors or Rotary Cutter
• Sewing Machine & Matching Thread (or pins/safety pins if doing a no-sew or pocket-corner method).


## 1. Step-by-Step Instructions to Make a Fitted No-Fur Bedsheet (Without Elastic)

**Phase 1: Measure and Cut the Fabric**
1. Measure your mattress: Note the length, width, and thickness (depth).
2. Calculate fabric size: Add the mattress dimensions plus the side depth (thickness) and an extra tuck/hem allowance (usually about 2 to 4 inches per side) plus a 1-inch seam allowance.
3. Cut square corners: At each of the four corners of your large fabric rectangle, cut out a square notch whose size matches your mattress depth plus seam allowance (e.g., if depth is 6 inches, cut roughly 7-to-8-inch squares from each corner).

**Phase 2: Sew the Corner Pockets (Box Shape)**
1. Pin the corners: Fold the fabric with the right sides together, bringing the cut edges of each corner square together.
2. Stitch the seams: Sew a straight stitch along each of the four cut corner edges to form a 3D box-like fitted pocket that hugs the mattress corners.
3. Reinforce or finish edges: Zigzag or overlock the raw edges inside the corners so they do not fray in the wash.

**Phase 3: Create Corner Holders or Hem the Edges**
Instead of a continuous rubber elastic band (which can wear out or trap fur), you can anchor a no-fur sheet cleanly:
1. Hem the perimeter: Fold the outer raw edges of the sheet under by 1/4 inch, then 1/2 inch, and topstitch all the way around the edge.
2. Add fabric corner pockets or straps: As an alternative to elastic garter straps, sew heavy-duty cotton triangle flaps or diagonal corner bands inside the underside of the sheet corners to grip the mattress tightly underneath.
